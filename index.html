Forthcoming Absence(s) from Rehearsal(s)

NSCI Members are expected to try to attend all rehearsals. However it is understood that this is not always possible. NCSI management will always try to work around individual issues - particularly last-minute emergencies.

Please communicate absences to the Choir Manager ahead of time whenever possible. We understand that circumstances change, but late drop-outs can make the planning and running of the NCSI really challenging.

Forthcoming Absence(s) from Performance(s)

NSCI Members are expected to commit to all or most of the concert performances scheduled in a season in order to retain their place in the choir. (Taking part in half or less of the concerts in a season will lead to a discussion regarding your continuing membership). However NSCI Management fully acknowledge that members will sometimes be compelled to miss a performance. They will always try to accommodate individual issues, particularly last-minute emergencies.

If you know you will be unable to take part in a forthcoming performance it is essential that the Choir Manager be advised at the earliest possible opportunity. The Chorus Director needs to know to what extent the choir may be operating in any forthcoming concert with somewhat reduced numbers. Late drop-outs make that sort of planning really challenging. Accordingly, such drop-outs must be avoided except when they arise by virtue of genuinely unforeseen & pressing circumstances.

If you wish you can complete this form to generate a pre-populated email, which you can then send to the Choir Manager, regarding any forthcoming performances you will be unable to take part in.

If you are unable to participate in a forthcoming performance, you are still welcome (and actively encouraged) to come nonetheless to as many rehearsals as you can. This helps keep the sound, musicianship & social culture of the choir alive and developing. It also helps build the institutional repertoire memory of the NCSI. But typically we expect to have only those singers who intend performing in a concert taking part in the final Wednesday choir-only rehearsal before concert week, and attending the rehearsals with the conductor during concert week.

Privacy/Data Protection

1.
This online NSCI Member Information Hub (“the App”) has been developed, on his own initiative, by Kevin O’Connell: an individual member of the NSCI. To contact him please click here.
2.
An Ceoláras Náisiúnta (the National Concert Hall) has not been involved in the development of the App. Neither is it involved in any control, maintenance or monitoring of it.
3.
The App is not intended to be used for gathering or disseminating anyone's personal data. No such data is collected when NSCI members choose whether or not to use the App. Similarly, no logs are maintained regarding anyone’s use of it.
4.
The NSCI Chorus Director and Choir Manager have both undertaken that no personal data will be uploaded by them to the “Current Notes” section of the App.
5.
NSCI Members remain perfectly free to choose not to download or use the App. Information regarding the NSCI's rehearsal & performance will continue to be communicated to choir members in exactly the same way as it was prior to the development of the App.
